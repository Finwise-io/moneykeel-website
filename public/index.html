A personal starting point

Where you are changes what matters most.

Your goals look different at 45 than they do at 70. aMoneyKeel is designed to put the questions that matter to you first, and help you explore your next step.

Building your future

Know if you're on track.

When you're earning and saving, your view emphasizes what's growing, what's protected, and what to save next.

Living in retirement

Make your savings work for your life.

After you retire, the focus shifts from accumulation to a monthly paycheck and choices that affect how long your money may last.

  • What's my retirement paycheck?Review income from Social Security, pensions and savings withdrawals.
  • Social Security: age 62, 67 or 70?Explore how different claim ages change monthly income and withdrawal needs.
  • Could I afford to spend more?Test higher spending against a range of future market outcomes, not a guarantee.

Two people. Two very different money questions.

Follow these illustrative journeys to see how the same app can help people at different points in life. Alex and Maria are fictional examples.

A

Alex, 46

Working • building savings and wealth
  1. Start with the whole pictureAlex reviews cash, investments, a mortgage and other debts to understand net worth.
  2. Separate saving from growthThe investment view distinguishes new contributions from what the investments actually earned.
  3. Check the rainy-day cushionAlex compares available cash with core monthly costs and notices where more reserve could help.
  4. Try a retirement goalAlex adjusts savings and retirement age to see what it may take to reach a desired date.
Alex's question: “What should I do this year to stay on track?”

Maria, 68

Retired • turning savings into income
  1. Understand the monthly paycheckMaria reviews Social Security, pension income and the amount needed from savings.

Questions

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Is aMoneyKeel available now?

The iPhone app is in private TestFlight beta. You can request access by emailing beta@moneykeel.ai; invitations are not automatic.

Can it automatically connect to my bank?

Manual entry and some imports are part of the documented app. Automatic bank linking is not confirmed as available in this beta, so we don't promise it here.

Does it give financial advice?

It provides information and planning estimates, not personalized investment, tax, or legal advice.

Are the numbers in these images real?

No. The website images are product design mockups containing example data, not a real customer's financial information.
